Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Roy Carmelli at roy-carmelli-portfolio.vercel.app. Reading the site requires no account and sets no tracking cookies. The only part that handles personal data is the optional leaderboard for the Roy Runner mini-game, and only if you choose to sign in with Google.

Short version: if you sign in, the leaderboard shows your first name, the first letter of your last name and your best score. Your e-mail address and profile photo are never stored. Ask and your data is deleted.

If you don't sign in

Your best score is saved only in your own browser (localStorage) so the game can show it next time. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

If you sign in with Google

Google shares your basic profile with the site. From it, the site stores:

  • Your Google account identifier, a number Google assigns to your account (not your e-mail). It is used only to recognise you as the same player next time.
  • A public display name made of your first name and last initial, for example "Roy C.".
  • Your best score and when you set it.
  • While you play, the time your current run started, used to reject impossible scores. It is deleted when the run ends.

The site does not store your e-mail address, your full last name, your profile photo or any Google token, and it never asks Google for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other data.

Signing in sets one cookie, a signed session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is HttpOnly (page scripts can't read it) and is used for nothing else.

What is public

Your display name ("Roy C.") and best score appear on the leaderboard, which anyone visiting the site can see. Nothing else about you is shown.

Who processes the data

  • Google handles the sign-in itself.
  • Vercel hosts the site and runs its server code. Like any web host it keeps short-lived request logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Neon hosts the leaderboard database, in the EU (Frankfurt).

The data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising or profiling.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Leaderboard entries are kept until you ask for them to be removed or the leaderboard is shut down. To delete your entry, e-mail roy.y.carmelli@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with; it is removed within 30 days, usually much sooner. Signing out deletes the session cookie from your browser. You can also remove the site's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

The leaderboard is not directed at children under 13, and they should not sign in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
